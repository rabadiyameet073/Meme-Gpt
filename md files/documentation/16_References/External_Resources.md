## Research Papers

| Paper | Authors | Year | Relevance |
|---|---|---|---|
| Sentence-BERT | Reimers & Gurevych | 2019 | Foundation for MiniLM embeddings |
| Learning Transferable Visual Models (CLIP) | Radford et al. | 2021 | Vision-language alignment for image embeddings |
| BLIP: Bootstrapping Language-Image Pre-training | Li et al. | 2022 | Image captioning for meme descriptions |
| MTEB: Massive Text Embedding Benchmark | Muennighoff et al. | 2023 | Embedding model comparison framework |
| Efficient Estimation of Word Representations (Word2Vec) | Mikolov et al. | 2013 | Foundational vector representation concepts |
| Attention Is All You Need | Vaswani et al. | 2017 | Transformer architecture underlying all models |
